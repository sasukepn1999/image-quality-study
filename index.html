Image quality study

Thank you for taking part. You will see 20 pairs of AI-generated images. It takes about 8–10 minutes.

Rate each image on its own, answering two questions:

  1. Visual quality, from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).
  2. Visual artifacts: does the image contain noticeable artifacts, such as distorted faces or bodies, broken or extra limbs and objects, noise, blotches or colour bands? Yes or No.

There are no right or wrong answers. Click any image to see it at full size.

Your answers are anonymous: we record only a random ID and your ratings.
